01 / The idea

Follow what is promised.
Then see what happened.

Observatório de Fiscalização Pública is an application designed to make public management easier to follow and analyse.

The goal is to connect what political parties and elected officials propose with the plans, budgets, projects, contracts and execution that follow — creating a structured view of what was intended, what received resources, what was carried out and what remains to be done.

The approach is designed to work at local, regional and national levels, starting with local government and expanding the same model to other public institutions.

03 / The model

Connect the dots.
Don't lose the evidence.

The project's conceptual model links the different stages of public action so that a conclusion can be understood in context.

The principle is to separate Fact → Source / Evidence → Calculation → Analysis → Evaluation / flag for investigation, rather than mixing raw information with conclusions.

04 / Current implementation

Already more than
a concept.

The current implementation is a Django application that structures entities such as commitments, actions, evidence, indicators, plans, contracts and financial execution.

Its document-processing pipeline includes PDF → OCR → extraction → normalisation → code reconstruction → conservative association → hierarchical validation → persistence.

The initial scope focuses on the União das Freguesias de Póvoa de Santa Iria e Forte da Casa, with the broader model intended for expansion to other public institutions.

05 / The experiment

From public information
to a traceable analysis.

The interesting product challenge is not simply putting more public information in one place. It is creating enough structure for different pieces of evidence to be connected without losing their origin.

Where the available evidence is insufficient, the system is designed to preserve the raw information rather than make an arbitrary attribution. That makes uncertainty part of the product instead of hiding it.

The long-term idea is to make it easier to understand what was proposed, what was planned, how resources were used, what was executed and what was not.

06 / Current status

A project about making public execution visible.

The Observatório is an active personal project. The current implementation is focused on building and validating the underlying evidence and financial-processing model before expanding its scope.
