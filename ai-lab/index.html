The workflow

Idea → conversation → prototype.

One of the things I find most interesting is the change in distance between having an idea and being able to test it. Instead of treating AI as a black box, I use the interaction itself as part of the product process.

The important part is not simply generating code. It is defining the problem, communicating intent, reviewing the result, making decisions and iterating until the product starts to make sense.

01

Define

Describe the problem, goal and constraints.

02

Explore

Use AI to challenge ideas, structure possibilities and investigate approaches.

03

Build

04

05

Repeat until the experiment produces something useful.

Tools & approaches

Different tools.
Different questions.

The goal is not to collect AI tools. It is to understand which approach makes sense for a particular problem.

Some experiments work better with a local model, others benefit from a stronger cloud model, and some simply don't need AI. The lab is about finding that boundary through actual experimentation.

The point of the lab

Learn what AI
actually changes.

I'm not interested in using AI because it is fashionable. I'm interested in understanding what becomes possible when AI becomes another tool in the product development process.
